Rewire Your Brain
==========================================
🔥 Chapter 1: Firing the Right Cells Together

👉 “Jo neurons saath-saath fire karte hain, wo saath-saath wire ho jaate hain.”
Matlab: Jo cheez aap baar-baar karte ho, wahi aapka brain strong bana leta hai.

Step 1: Focus

Brain wahi strong karta hai jahan attention hoti hai

Jab aap distract hote ho → weak wiring

Jab full focus hota hai → strong wiring

📌 Example:
Agar aap coding seekhte waqt mobile side mein rakh ke karoge → slow progress
Agar 40 min deep focus → fast learning

Step 2: Effort

Brain ko change hone ke liye thoda struggle chahiye

Comfort zone mein brain grow nahi karta

📌 Example:
Leetcode ka hard question = brain gym 🏋️
Jitna effort → utni neural growth

Step 3: Effortlessness

Baar-baar practice se kaam automatic ho jaata hai

Starting mein hard → baad mein flow

📌 Example:
Pehle Java Streams confusing
Ab map/filter dekh ke hi samajh aa jaata

Step 4: Determination

Result slow hota hai, isliye consistency important

Brain wiring time leti hai

📌 Mantra:

Daily thoda thoda, par roz
====================================================================
😨 Chapter 2: Controlling Your Amygdala

👉 Amygdala = fear + stress + anxiety center

Jab Amygdala control mein nahi hoti:

Overthinking

Interview fear

“Mujhse nahi hoga” thoughts

Control kaise kare?

✔ Slow breathing (4-6 sec inhale, 6-8 sec exhale)
✔ Name the emotion: “Mujhe dar lag raha hai”
✔ Logic activate karo → problem ko steps mein todo

📌 Example:
Interview se pehle panic ❌
Interview ko questions + answers + examples mein break karo ✅

😊 Chapter 3: Creating Positive Mood

👉 Positive mood = fast learning + better memory

Step 1: Keep Smile on Face

Fake smile bhi brain ko signal deta hai: “Sab theek hai”

Step 2: Light Chemistry

Sunshine 🌞

Fresh air

Natural light

Ye dopamine & serotonin boost karta hai

Step 3: Aerobic Boosting

Walk, jogging, skipping

Blood + oxygen = brain power 🚀

📌 Sirf 20 min walk = focus + mood improve

Step 4: Creating Narratives

Apni life ko story samjho, failure ko villain nahi lesson banao

❌ “Main weak hoon”
✅ “Main abhi training phase mein hoon”

Step 5: Wiring Positive Thinking

Thoughts repeat honge → wiring strong hogi

Negative self-talk ko replace karo

📌 Replace Rule:

Negative → Neutral → Positive

===============================================================
🧠 Chapter 4: Cultivating Memory

👉 Memory = attention + emotion + repetition

Memory strong karne ke rules:

✔ Ek time pe ek cheez
✔ Visualize karo
✔ Explain karo kisi aur ko
✔ Spaced repetition

📌 Example:

Java concept padho

Uska example likho

Kisi ko samjhao (ya khud ko)
===================================================================
🔁 Chapter 5: Rewire the Mind in Healthy Habits

👉 Habits = autopilot of brain

Habit ka structure:

* Cue → Routine → Reward

📌 Example:

* Cue: Subah uthna

* Routine: 30 min DSA

* Reward: Coffee / Music

Rule:

* Habits ko chhota rakho

Environment ko change karo, willpower pe depend mat karo
======================================================================
🎯 Final Summary (One Line per Chapter)

1️⃣ Focus + effort = brain wiring
2️⃣ Fear ko observe karo, obey mat karo
3️⃣ Positive mood = fast learning
4️⃣ Memory grows by usage, not reading
5️⃣ Small habits daily = big brain change
